# Q2 operating review

Audience: Executive leadership team
Objective: Agree on the two operating priorities that protect growth while improving efficiency
Type: report
Tone: candid, concise, analytical
Visual direction: invent a board-credible visual thesis in which editorial conclusions lead financial evidence; avoid generic dashboards
Slides: 10
Language: English
Topics: performance metrics, revenue mix, adoption trend, cost bridge, customer health, operating priorities, next-quarter roadmap

Lead with the conclusion, explain what each chart means, and close with a decision and named next actions.
