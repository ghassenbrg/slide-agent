# V5 — LinkedIn launch / 45 seconds

## Final brief

User wants the launch energy and motion of slide-agent-launch.mp4, slide-agent-promo.mp4 and slide-agent-linkedin.mp4, tailored to LinkedIn. Rebuilt from their motion language rather than speeding up the previous cut. User's latest timing overrides the original 60-second brief: 40–50 seconds, target 45. Analytics always uses slide 1.

## Diagnosis and direction

V4 holds the same headline-over-card layout too long; the gradual music build and long setup dilute the announcement. Replace those pauses with purposeful action: finished output immediately, ask, build, five different uses, revise, deliver, try. Use 1–3-second internal beats and decisive transitions while letting each featured presentation settle for inspection. Keep installation instructions in the post and linked guide, not a tutorial segment in the film.

Use the original films' real-slide animation, card motion, emphatic type and light/dark contrast. Reframe for 1080×1350; enlarge content and keep safe areas. Groove and melodic pulse from frame zero, short transition accents, purposeful close-ups and accelerated assembly. The ending holds for reading; all key messages work without sound. No speed promise or invented interface: the brief and assembly are explicitly illustrative, the deck exports and revision are real.
