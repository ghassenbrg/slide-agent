I'm launching Slide Agent — an open-source project I've been building to help AI assistants create real, editable PowerPoint presentations.

Bring a brief and your source material. Your assistant shapes the story and design; Slide Agent handles layouts, text fitting, contrast checks and native PowerPoint output.

This 45-second announcement uses real presentations: system architecture, project reviews, analytics, product marketing and strategy roadmaps. Different subjects, different visual styles.

It also shows a real design revision. You can keep refining with your assistant, then open the .pptx and edit the text, charts and shapes yourself.
