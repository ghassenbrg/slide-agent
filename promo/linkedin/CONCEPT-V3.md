# LinkedIn launch — creative analysis and committed treatment

## Product truth

Slide Agent is an open-source presentation engine used by an AI assistant. The assistant directs narrative, concept, palette, typography and composition. The engine computes layout, fits text, checks contrast and writes native editable PowerPoint. The result can be reviewed and revised. It is not a separate autonomous chat app, an image generator, or a promise of instant generation.

Evidence: README; installation guide; actual showcase intents, computed scenes and exported PPTX packages. The architecture opening slide contains 170 native shape objects and zero embedded pictures. Its wiring is editable vector geometry, not a flattened diagram. The analytics example contains real chart parts and embedded data. Sample business metrics describe fictional example projects, not product performance.

## Audience and message

Primary LinkedIn audience: engineers, technical leads, product managers, consultants and founders who must explain complex work to colleagues. Secondary: people making business reviews and data-led presentations. Assume sound is off and attention is limited; this is a creative choice, not an unsupported claim about platform statistics.

The value proposition is a usable, editable presentation within the assistant workflow. Prioritize the concrete artifact over generic AI claims. Technical readers should recognize the quality of the architecture; nontechnical readers should immediately see a practical presentation tool.

## Diagnosis of the previous cuts

The original promo has dramatic contrast and motion but takes too long to reach a usable product explanation. V2 identifies the product immediately and is accurate, but its repeated white text panels and analytics-only middle feel like a walkthrough. The showcase collage is too small to inspect and the GitHub-only CTA misses the now-live official site.

## Concepts considered

1. A gallery of beautiful decks: strong visual variety, weak tool explanation; reject.
2. Old workflow versus AI: easy problem setup, uses valuable opening time on generic frustration; reject.
3. One complex idea, made presentable: architecture is the spine, workflow explains the tool, other real outputs demonstrate breadth; select.

## Committed concept: Complex ideas. Clear presentations.

Open on the product name and a beautiful real architecture slide. Pull back to the brief and the assistant's story/design choices. Follow the system diagram from gateway to orchestration; show editable native objects. Extend the same promise to business reviews, data and planning. Make installation approachable, then resolve to the product and official URL.

This is a launch announcement, not a demo of the fictional AI platform depicted in the sample. Keep Slide Agent visible; clearly label the main sample. A small 'Illustrated workflow' label distinguishes illustrative interaction from a recorded live generation session. No generated timing or speed claims.

## Art direction and motion

1080×1350, 30 fps. Midnight navy stage with icy white typography, cyan and violet accents drawn from the architecture example. Clean off-white section for the business/data/planning range creates contrast without changing the product identity. Large sans-serif headlines; monospace only for small labels and exact commands. No decorative typewriter delays or repeated word-by-word hooks.

Actual PowerPoint render PNGs are the visual authority. Camera motion zooms into useful detail, pauses to read, then returns. Selection overlays use the engine's coordinates. Transitions are short crossfades and matched slide movements; no arbitrary spinning or constant motion. Broad text remains readable at feed scale; details earn a larger crop. A subtle original electronic score supports motion; every essential message works without audio.
