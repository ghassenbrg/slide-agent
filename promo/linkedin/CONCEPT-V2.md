# Slide Agent — product announcement

## Creative diagnosis

The previous cut gave 25 of 60 seconds to five independently introduced slide styles. Repeated prompts, palettes and genre headings made the slides the subject of the film. A viewer could remember the examples without understanding the tool or the workflow.

The announcement needs to answer, in order: what is launching, what do I give it, what does it do with that input, what do I get, can I change it, and how do I start?

## Committed concept: “Your brief. A real presentation.”

Introduce Slide Agent by name on the first frame. Follow one analytics brief through a coherent presentation workflow. Use the existing five-slide analytics showcase, its real narrative/design language, its native chart and an actual accent-colour revision. Other showcase decks appear only as a short proof of range after the workflow is understood.

The assistant directs the narrative and design. Slide Agent builds native slides, fits text and verifies mechanical constraints. Keep these responsibilities explicit. The chat is a labelled workflow illustration, not an invented product interface or a recorded timed generation. No fabricated speed claims, no unreleased branding, no first-person authorship in the video.

## Locked storyboard — 60 seconds, 1080×1350

| Time | Narrative job | Picture and message |
|---|---|---|
| 0–4 | Announce immediately | “Introducing Slide Agent.” A compact brief flows into a real slide. “From your brief to editable PowerPoint.” |
| 4–11 | Explain the input | Brief and supplied metrics/notes in an illustrative assistant conversation. “Start with a brief. Bring your context.” |
| 11–18 | Show intelligence | The actual analytics deck's five story beats and editorial design language. “It shapes the story. Then the design.” |
| 18–27 | Explain the engine | Slides assemble from the engine's actual computed geometry; native chart and decision slide. Layout, text fitting, checks and .pptx output. |
| 27–33 | Prove the output | Select native text/chart; reveal actual embedded chart values. “Real PowerPoint. Text and charts you can edit.” |
| 33–39 | Show collaboration | Ask for a blue accent; show the actual rebuilt revision of the same analytics slide. “Ask for a change. Keep the conversation going.” |
| 39–44 | Prove breadth, briefly | Five real showcase covers in one compact composition, without independent genre tours. “For the work you present.” |
| 44–54 | Make trial possible | Accurate alternative VS Code / CLI setup, Node requirement and a sample ask. |
| 54–60 | Close with action | “Try Slide Agent.” Working GitHub address, install links in the post, MIT licence. |

## Visual and motion direction

Keep the established clean paper, blue and editorial typography. A persistent product lockup anchors the story. The brief, outline, design language and slide canvas have related geometry and move forward as one workflow. Short entrances and restrained UI sounds signal meaningful steps. The finished output is proof within the product narrative.

Use one actual sample family throughout the main journey: warm paper, Charter headings, Helvetica Neue body, coral data signal. In the revision scene, blue becomes the signal. This makes the before/after immediately legible without fabricating content changes.

The original score will be rescored to the new scene boundaries. The video carries its message with sound off. Personal authorship belongs in the accompanying LinkedIn post.

## Production and review gates

1. Preserve the previous cut.
2. Prepare the actual analytics scene/assets and a real colour revision using the engine.
3. Implement this locked narrative.
4. Check key frames at full size and feed scale, especially story-card readability, slide fidelity and setup text.
5. Render the full cut, verify codecs/colour/duration, decode fully and measure the audio mix.
6. Rewrite the post around the tool and workflow; keep verified installation/documentation links.
