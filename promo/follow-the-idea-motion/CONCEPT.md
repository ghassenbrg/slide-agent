# Ask. See. Shape. Present.

A new tool-focused revision, led by the light Atlas project deck. The viewer follows a single task through an AI conversation and a real slide preview, asks for a concrete change, then takes away a PowerPoint. Deck content is visual evidence, not the subject of the narration. 52 seconds; 1080 × 1350; 30 fps.

## Motion and audio direction

Smooth damped springs (damping 18–24, stiffness 110–180), staggered masked typography, UI typing/submission/selection, continuous camera pushes and pullbacks, shared slide/icon match moves, quick curtain transitions followed by readable holds. No arbitrary endless zooming. Each cursor press has a synchronized physical click; typing and soft paper/air cues support actions. Original procedural music is reused at a speech-supporting level, with short energy lifts during gaps. Voice is newly scripted and regenerated. Caption sentences remain readable and steady.

The two user-named references were inspected as motion references: adopt their cause-and-effect UI actions, layered builds, camera shifts and spring typography. Do not reuse their scripts, scene order, technical explanation, static templates or their compositions. This revision has a newly authored action sequence, Atlas as hero, and a standalone Remotion entry.
